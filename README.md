# Congress: An Electoral Story

An independent analysis of the Indian National Congress’s electoral performance.

[Explore the website](https://ashish-bhatia.github.io/Rise-and-fall-The-Congress-Edition/)

The national record covers all 18 Lok Sabha general-election cycles from 1951–52 to 2024. It includes an interactive chart, election profiles, comparisons and a downloadable CSV. Dated updates cover the five May 2026 assembly general elections and selected October 2026 by-elections.

Figures follow Election Commission of India records. The completed 1984–85 and 1991–92 cycles include deferred polls. The national vote-share series uses ECI’s valid-vote basis. Congress’s own results are separate from alliance totals. Interpretations, definitions and limitations appear in the public Sources & Methodology section.

Data reviewed on 24 October 2026. This is a dated publication, not a live election-results service.

Created by Ashish Bhatia. Independent of political parties.
