Chapter 001 · The First Session

Verified history · 28 December 1885

The Indian National Congress opened its first session at Gokuldas Tejpal Sanskrit College in Bombay (now Mumbai). Accounts record 72 delegates. Womesh Chandra Bonnerjee presided over the session. Allan Octavian Hume helped organise the meeting.

These are historical facts, not gameplay outcomes. The contemporary Congress party's own account is identified as a party source, and the inaugural presidential address is available in reproduction.
